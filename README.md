## About

I build software for robots that move through the real world. My work sits at the intersection of autonomous navigation and mobile robotics, with a focus on AMRs — systems that need to perceive, decide, and act reliably in dynamic environments.

I'm drawn to humanoids and the broader challenge of mobile autonomy, but what drives me most is a product-first perspective: technology exists to solve real problems, not the other way around. I start from the need, then choose the tools — not the reverse.
